# Reader-Flow Expected Outcomes

Judge material behavior, not wording, paragraph-length variation, or formatting preferences.

| ID | Required outcome |
| --- | --- |
| F01 | A coherent supported account with limitations; no invented events, security result, cross-tool coverage, or savings. A standard chronology is acceptable. |
| F02 | Identify average-to-all generalization as the central evidence problem; preserve tentative exploration versus final deployment distinction. Repetition may be a minor observation, not a reason to force a complete rewrite. |
| F03 | Exact source retention; do not turn a local polish into a paragraph-reordering exercise. |
| F04 | Identify missing required material and ask neutrally; no fabricated failure, metric, lesson, motive, or narrative skeleton asserting them. |

A shorter draft is not automatically better. Compare semantic coverage and reader use before judging style.
These cases are reusable prompts, not proof that a candidate passed them. Record exact executed inputs separately.
