## Claim Boundary

These fixtures can establish selection, authority, reference-role, Impression Map, mode-routing, targeted-revision,
capability-fallback, privacy, host-precedence, and claim-boundary behavior.

They cannot establish likeness quality, visual excellence, deterministic output, runtime support, locale support, or
product maturity. Those claims require frozen public-safe portrait fixtures, actual image generation, human review, and
runtime-specific installation and invocation evidence.
