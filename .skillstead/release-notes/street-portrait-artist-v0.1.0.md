## street-portrait-artist 0.1.0

This initial Experimental release turns permitted portrait references into one of two visibly authored character
interpretations. `Street Caricature` extracts one kind, witty relational idea and redraws each face with open-paper
planes, near-monochrome ink or graphite, decisive black masses, and at most a tiny muted spot color. `Romance
Watercolor` uses the same identity grammar for a delicate pen-and-watercolor character portrait with restrained
idealization and a softly retained environment.

Both modes begin with an `Impression Map` of the head frame, feature relationships, expression, outer anchors, and one
primary anchor. Supplied portraits, analysis, and outputs remain task-scoped and cannot become public examples or
persistent character profiles without separate permission.

## Evidence And Limits

- Package: `skills/street-portrait-artist/`
- Tag: `street-portrait-artist/v0.1.0`
- Public evidence: two synthetic `Twin Portrait` cases; all six gallery PNG files are `1080 x 1350 px`
- Runtime support: ChatGPT and Codex are `Validation pending`; fresh tagged installation, discovery, invocation,
  reference-image, fallback, and output-delivery evidence are not yet accepted
- Compatibility or migration: initial release; install the complete skill folder
- Known limitations: likeness and image generation are non-deterministic; exact raster export depends on the active
  image surface; review the final artwork before publication

The versioned unit is the package above. GitHub's source archive is a snapshot of the whole repository at the tagged
